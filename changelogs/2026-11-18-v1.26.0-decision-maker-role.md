# Add decision-maker role and decision-making routing (v1.26.0)

Separates orchestration/coordination from judgment authority so compositions
with a lighter orchestrator (such as `opus-fable` or `sol-astra`) route
decisions to the designated decision-maker instead of deciding in the
orchestrator pane.

- **New `roles.decision-maker` and `assignments.decision-making`**: `skills/orchestration/roles.yaml`
  places `decision-maker: orchestrator` right after `planning-design` (preserving
  top-level `orchestrator` above `roles:` and `generalist` last), and adds the
  `decision-making` work task (`role: decision-maker`, `mode: orchestrator`) to
  retain legacy behavior by default.
- **Profiles updated**: `opus-fable` binds `decision-maker` to `Fable 5.2` (max)
  and `sol-astra` binds `decision-maker` to `GPT-6.2-Astra` (max), both with
  `assignments.decision-making.mode: delegate`. The four `*-ultra` and `*-solo`
  presets bind `decision-maker: orchestrator`. `.herdrpowers/profiles/README.md`
  is updated to match.
- **Skills, workflows, briefs, and docs**: Routes plan resolution, review-finding
  acceptance and disagreement adjudication, preflight/mid-execution/breaker
  rulings, "Declined to judge" dispositions, and final synthesis judgment through
  `decision-making` (`decision-maker`), adding
  `skills/pane-driven-development/decision-maker-brief.md` for delegated
  decision passes while keeping orchestration, lifecycle, worktrees, and
  mechanical integration in the active owner and user decisions with the user.
- **Tests and manifests**: Advances pack manifests and `package.json` to
  `1.26.0` and expands `tests/orchestration-profiles/profiles.test.mjs` to cover
  role ordering, profile bindings, task tables, and removal of hardcoded
  orchestrator-decides rules.
