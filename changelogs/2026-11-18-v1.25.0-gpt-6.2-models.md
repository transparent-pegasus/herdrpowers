# GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol model pins (v1.25.0)

OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol on 2026-11-17. Shipped profiles
now pin `GPT-6.2-Sol` where they pinned `GPT-6.1-Sol` and `GPT-6.2-Astra`
where they pinned `GPT-6-Astra`. The strings match the live Codex footer,
verified 2026-11-18 on codex-cli 0.167.0 in a herdr pane:
`-m gpt-6.2-sol -c model_reasoning_effort=max` shows `GPT-6.2-Sol max`, and
`-m gpt-6.2-astra` shows `GPT-6.2-Astra max`.

The shipped `roles.yaml` defaults move the OpenCode reviewer to
`GPT-6.2 Astra` and the OpenCode coder to `GPT-6.2 Sol` (OpenCode 2.0.32
footer `Build · GPT-6.2 Astra Personal / OpenCode`; model picker
`GPT-6.2 Sol`). Fallbacks gain `codex@GPT-6.2-Astra: []` and
`codex@GPT-6.2-Sol: [codex]`; the previous-release keys stay so configs still
pinned to GPT-6-Astra or GPT-6.1-Sol keep their substitutes.

Docs and examples (`init`, `docs/roles.md`, the profiles contract, and the
sibling-pane references) use the new strings. Footer-reader fixtures and
mismatch tests keep their recorded strings: they test parsing and mismatch,
not the current release.
