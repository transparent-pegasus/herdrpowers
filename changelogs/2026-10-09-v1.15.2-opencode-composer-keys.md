# v1.15.2 — OpenCode composer keys

## Summary

`composer-submit.sh` never reset an opencode pane's session. opencode 1.18.35
opens its slash popup on the pasted `/clear`, and its `esc` closes that popup
and wipes the composer along with it, so the helper's `enter` submitted an
empty line. Every delegation landed in the previous conversation — breaking the
fresh-session property that reset-backed delegation relies on — while the
helper still exited `0`. `probe-composer.sh` passed its reset step anyway,
because that step only read the helper's exit code. The probe now checks that
the reset took effect, and the helper resets opencode with its new-session
keybind.

## Details

- **Reset.** For an `opencode` pane the helper sends `ctrl+x`, then `n` (the
  default `session_new` keybind under leader `ctrl+x`) instead of `/clear` +
  `esc` + `enter`. No popup is involved. codex, cursor, and grok keep the
  shared sequence unchanged. `enter` on the open popup also works — it runs
  `/new`, the top match for `clear` — but it depends on the popup having
  rendered and on its ranking; the keybind depends on neither.
- **Probe step 8 (new): the reset starts a fresh conversation.** Step 5's
  instruction now names a probe word. After the next helper reset the probe
  asks for it, with a random reply tail so no earlier run's reply can match. A
  fresh session answers `NONE`; a session that was never reset still knows the
  word. Step 4 now reports only that the reset was sent. Interrupt and survival
  move to steps 9 and 10.
- **Interrupt.** opencode needs `esc` twice: the first press only arms "esc
  again to interrupt". The probe's interrupt step sends both for opencode;
  nothing else in the payload interrupts a pane.
- **Post-marker wait.** The transport skill's example waited with
  `herdr agent wait --until idle`. herdr reports work that finished unseen as
  `done` until someone focuses it, so that wait sat out its whole timeout after
  a delegation to an unwatched pane. It is now `--until idle --until done`,
  matching the workflow step's own "idle or done".
- The Verified keys table notes opencode on the reset, dismiss-popup, and
  interrupt rows, and the hand sequence gains its opencode substitution.
- Version 1.15.2 across the four manifests and `package.json`.

## Verification

On herdr 0.9.3, against opencode 1.18.35 in the pane below the orchestrator,
with opencode's own session store (`opencode.db`) as the ground truth:

- 1.15.1's probe: 8/9. Step 8 (interrupt) failed and step 4 passed, yet the
  store held one session containing both probe instructions — no reset.
- By hand: pasting `/clear` opens the popup with `/new` on top; `esc` empties
  the composer; `ctrl+x` `n` opens a new session from inside a conversation;
  one `esc` on a running task shows "esc again to interrupt" while the task
  keeps going, and two stop it; `@types/node` and an email address pasted
  mid-sentence open no popup and survive `esc`.
- New probe with 1.15.1's helper: step 8 FAIL (the agent answered with the
  probe word), everything else PASS — the new check catches this bug.
- New probe with the fixed helper: 10/10 PASS. The store shows three sessions
  with one instruction each, and the reset check answered `NONE`.
- One delegation under the task contract (absolute working directory, report
  file, split marker, no-git-reset clause): marker matched, the report was
  complete and correct, and the pane made no edits. `agent wait --until idle`
  then timed out on the pane's `done`; `--until idle --until done` returned at
  once.

## Not verified

- Probe step 8 has not run against codex, cursor, or grok: no pane of those
  types was in the orchestrator's tab. Their helper path is unchanged, but step
  4 never verified their reset either, so re-run the probe on each.
